# Experiment Log

## Week 1
**Experiment Name:** `Week1_LinkedIn_Activation`  
**Status:** Baseline complete, experiment scheduled

### Objective
Measure whether intentional LinkedIn posting increases traffic and engagement on quinnreams.com.

### Hypothesis
Posting a project-focused and insight-focused LinkedIn post with tracked links will increase sessions and engagement compared to baseline.

### Primary Metric
- Sessions

### Secondary Metrics
- Project page views
- Project clicks
- GitHub clicks
- Resume clicks
- Email clicks

### Baseline Window
- 2026-03-18 to 2026-03-24

### Experiment Plan
- **Post 1:** Wednesday at 10:00 AM
- **Post 2:** Saturday at 10:00 AM

### Campaign Names
- `mil_wk1_project_post`
- `mil_wk1_insight_post`

### Controls
- No major site redesign during experiment window
- No additional planned promotion outside LinkedIn
- Same posting time for both posts

### Baseline Summary
Week 1 baseline traffic was low and inconsistent. Most days fell in a low-session range, with one notably high day that appears to be an outlier. Traffic was primarily direct and engagement was sporadic.

### Notes
The baseline period confirms that the site currently does not have a stable, controllable traffic source. This makes the LinkedIn activation test a useful first experiment.
