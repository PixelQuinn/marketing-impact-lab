# Week 1 Baseline and LinkedIn Test Setup

## Reporting Window
**Start Date:** 2026-03-18  
**End Date:** 2026-03-24

## Overview
This report documents the Week 1 baseline period for Marketing Impact Lab. The goal of this period was to measure natural portfolio traffic and engagement before launching the first controlled LinkedIn experiment.

## Objective
Establish a realistic baseline for:
- sessions
- traffic source / medium
- project page views
- project clicks
- GitHub clicks
- resume clicks
- email clicks

## Baseline Observations
Week 1 traffic was low and inconsistent. Most days fell into a low-session range, with one unusually high day that should be treated as an outlier rather than a normal baseline value.

Traffic was primarily direct, with no consistent controllable source. Engagement behavior was also inconsistent, suggesting that site activity was mostly incidental rather than driven by structured promotion.

## Initial Interpretation
The baseline period suggests that the portfolio site currently operates in a low-signal environment. This is useful for experimentation because even small changes in traffic and interaction behavior may be meaningful when tied to a controlled input.

The baseline also shows that engagement metrics should be interpreted as interaction counts rather than unique users, since one visitor may trigger multiple events.
